FAQ & Help

Is this free?

RobuNexa is completely free.

How to withdraw?

Go to the Withdraw tab, enter the amount you want (min 50 R$) and your Gamepass link.

When do I get paid?

Withdrawals are processed manually to ensure safety. It usually takes 24-72 hours.

How do Shortlinks work?

Click 'Start Task', visit the link, find the verification code on the destination site, and paste it back here to get paid.

Why is my balance 0?

New accounts start with 0. You can earn by completing tasks, inviting friends, or claiming daily bonuses.

Can I use multiple accounts?

No. Using multiple accounts or VPNs is strictly prohibited and will result in a permanent ban.

I didn't get Robux?

If your status is 'Approved' but you don't see Robux, check your Roblox 'Pending Robux' transactions. It takes 3-7 days for Roblox to release funds.
